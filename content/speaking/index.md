I am an experienced public speaker and am available for talks at schools, universities, conferences, science festivals, and media appearances. I bring genuine enthusiasm for astronomy and a commitment to making science accessible to everyone.

I have received a **Queensland Museum Women in STEM Breaking Barriers Award** and am a passionate advocate for underrepresented groups in science, including queer and disabled scientists.

---

## Topics I speak on

**Astronomy & Astrophysics**
- Supermassive black holes and active galactic nuclei
- Galaxy evolution and galaxy collisions
- Changing-look AGN: black holes that switch on and off
- The Rubin Observatory / LSST and the future of time-domain astronomy
- How astronomers use integral field spectroscopy

**Careers & Broader Themes**
- What it's like to be an astronomer
- Women, LGBTQ+, and disabled people in STEM
- Navigating an academic career
